How it works

From registration to rescue — four simple steps

No app to install, no complicated sign-up. The whole flow runs in the browser for this demo.

Step 1

Register once

Donors submit blood group, phone, and location — takes under a minute.

Step 2

Stay listed

Profile stays in the directory, marked available or resting after a recent donation.

Step 3

Request posted

A patient's family posts an emergency request with blood group, hospital, and urgency.

Step 4

Instant match

Matching available donors surface immediately with one-tap call and SMS.
